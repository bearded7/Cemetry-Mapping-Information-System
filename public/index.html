Instant name search

A full-text index finds a grave the moment you type a name — first, last, or maiden — even with partial matches.

Live GPS & walking directions

Tap a result and the map routes you from your current location to the exact grave using open routing data.

Admin-reviewed submissions

Add a grave in three steps

Family members and caretakers can contribute — every entry is checked before it goes live.

01

Stand at the grave

Open “Add a grave” on your phone and let it read your device's GPS position, or drop a pin on the map.

02

Fill in what you know

Name, dates, plot reference, and up to five photos of the headstone.

03

An admin reviews it

Once approved, it's searchable and routable for everyone. Rejected entries come back with a reason.
